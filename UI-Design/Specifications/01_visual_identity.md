# Zellige: Visual Identity

Status: direction approved by the lead (sample review). This document is the source of truth for colour, type, shape and layout. Where it disagrees with `docs/phase3/08_ui_ux_design_spec.md` (for example on palette), this document wins.

All art is original and procedurally generated by the scripts in `UI-Design/Generated/tools/`. No reference captures or existing deck artwork were used as input.

---

## 1. Mood

A premium card table at night: deep navy and royal-blue felt, framed by silver zellige tilework, lit softly from the centre. Gold and orange are accents, used sparingly. The centre of the table stays calm so the cards carry the scene.

Key words: **calm, crafted, legible, warm-on-cool.**

## 3. Typography

No text is baked into exported assets except card rank numerals. All dynamic text uses TextMeshPro. Recommended free fonts (SIL Open Font License, full Latin and Latin-Extended coverage, suitable for TMP SDF atlases):

| Role | First choice | Alternatives | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display / call-outs ("Ronda!", "Missa!", "Derba!", result titles) | **Marcellus** | Cinzel (caps only), Cormorant Garamond Bold | Classical, carved feel that matches the zellige frame |
| Names, scores, numbers | **Lora** SemiBold/Bold | Crimson Pro, Cormorant Garamond | Serif with strong lining figures, close to the card numerals |
| Labels, buttons, body, settings | **Nunito Sans** | Source Sans 3, Inter | Clean and very legible at small sizes |

Sizes at the 1920×1080 reference:

| Element | Font | Size | Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Call-out | Marcellus | 80–96 | gold_hi fill, 3 px gold-ink outline, soft shadow |
| Dialog title | Marcellus | 56 | gold_hi or text |
| Player name | Lora Bold | 34 | text |
| Score digits | Lora Bold | 34–40 | gold_hi, centred in capsule |
| Section label | Nunito Sans Bold, all caps | 18–20 | text_2, letter-spacing +4 to +5 |
| Button label | Nunito Sans ExtraBold, all caps | 28–32 | ink on gold / text on navy |
| Body / settings | Nunito Sans Regular | 24–26 | text / text_2 |

Minimum on-screen text size is 18 px at the reference resolution.

**Card numerals:** rasterised from Cambria Bold (`C:\Windows\Fonts\cambriab.ttf`, Microsoft) into the card PNGs only. Cambria is **not** shipped as a font asset. To avoid any licensing doubt in the final build, rerun the card generator with `--font <path to Lora-Bold.ttf>`. The layout auto-fits glyph width, so no code change is needed. The mock-up uses Cambria and Segoe UI as stand-ins; they are not deliverables.

## 4. Card design language

* **Size:** 360 × 540 px (2:3), corner radius 26 px, transparent corners. In-game display is 120–176 px wide (table about 140 px, hand about 176 px).
* **Stock:** vertical ivory gradient (`ivory_hi` to `ivory`), a 3 px `ivory_edge` outer line, and a 2 px `ivory_line` inner frame inset 13 px.
* **Corner indices:** the rank numeral (in the suit's dark value) sits top-left with a small suit pip beneath it, and the same index is rotated 180° bottom-right. Glyph cap height is about 46 px (about 18 px at 140 px display width).
* **Pip cards 1–7:** fixed, symmetric pip grids. The ace has one large symbol on a pale suit-tinted 8-point star with two gold diamonds.
* **Court cards 10 / 11 / 12 (sota / caballo / rey):** an emblematic composition rather than figure illustration. A large suit symbol sits on a suit-tinted 8-point star medallion (dark outline plus gold inner line), with a double gold-and-ivory frame. A distinguishing ornament sits above the medallion (10 = gold star rosette, 11 = silver horseshoe, 12 = gold crown with orange jewels), and 1, 2 or 3 gold diamonds below it give a second at-a-glance rank cue.
* **Back:** ivory rim around a navy field tiled with royal-blue 8-point stars outlined in silver, gold studs at the cross points, and a central silver-and-gold medallion. It reads the same upside down.
* **Line weights** at 360 px: outer edge 3, frame 2, symbol outline about 2.2% of symbol size, court medallion 4.

## 6. Pattern language

* **Base motif:** the 8-point star made from two squares rotated 45° (inner radius ratio 0.765). Rows of stars on a square grid create the classic star-and-cross zellige.
* **Where it appears:** the table frame band (40 px pitch), the faint felt lattice (120 px pitch, fades to zero in the centre), the card back (48 px pitch at 360 px), medallions, corner stars, and the avatar halo.
* **Restraint:** pattern lives at edges and on backs. Card faces and the play area stay plain. Pattern is never placed behind text without a solid panel underneath.
* **Accent shapes:** small diamonds (squares rotated 45°) as separators and rank markers. Gold 8-point rosettes as terminators.
* **No** figurative religious symbols, calligraphy or text-like ornament.

---

## 7. Spacing, scaling and hierarchy

* **Canvas Scaler:** Scale With Screen Size, reference 1920 × 1080, Match = 1 (height). Landscape only.
* **Spacing grid:** 8 px, with 4 px allowed for fine adjustments. Common steps are 8 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48.
* **Safe frame:** the decorative band covers 0–68 px on every edge. Keep interactive UI at least 88 px from each screen edge (20 px inside the frame).
* **Hierarchy, top to bottom:** call-out, then the selected card, then hand and table cards, then the turn indicator and scores, then panels, labels and decoration.
* **Touch and click targets:** at least 64 × 64 px (round buttons are 68).
